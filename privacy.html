Privacy

Your details, kept simple.

This page explains what we do with the details you send us through this website.

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms: your name, phone number, email, city, and what you tell us about your event, franchise interest, feedback or job application, plus any CV you choose to attach.

Why we use it

To reply to you: to call you back about catering, a franchise enquiry or a job, and to improve our food and service from your feedback. We do not sell or rent your details, and we do not send marketing messages unless you ask us to.

Who sees it

The Anna Paatram team that handles your request. Form messages are delivered by email.

How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it to deal with your request, and no longer than required by law.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what you have sent us, message us on WhatsApp at +91 70213 14620 and we will act on it.
