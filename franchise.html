A day in the kitchen

Sixteen hours. No ready batter. No soda.

Soak

Rice and urad dal, in the ratio on the card. Nothing else goes in.

Grind

Stone-ground in the wet grinder, not blitzed. Sixteen hours from soak to tawa.

Ferment

No soda, no ready batter. It rises on its own, or it does not go out.

Serve

Every Tier 1 dish named with its region. Three chutneys on the tray. Podi taught, with ghee.

Questions partners ask

Straight answers.

Do I need to know South Indian cooking?

No. Two trained chefs are deputed to your kitchen. You run a business: hire, keep the counter clean, bank the cash, read the reports.

Where does the batter come from?

Your own kitchen, every day. Rice and urad dal, soaked, stone-ground and fermented sixteen hours on site. No ready batter, no soda, nothing shipped from a central kitchen.

What is the royalty?

6% of net sales ex-GST, billed monthly. Under FOCO, a management fee is agreed per site on top.

What is not in the investment figures?

Rent deposit, GST (18% on fee and royalty), major civil work, power back-up, and the extra cost of a larger area than the standard formats.

How long is the agreement?

Five years, with your territory protected for the term.
