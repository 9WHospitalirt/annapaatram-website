The menu · 100% pure vegetarian · Jain on request

Karnataka. Andhra. Tamil Nadu.

We don't serve South Indian food. We serve Karnataka, Andhra and Tamil Nadu, and we tell you which is which. Every dosa and idli is ground on our sixteen-hour batter. Three chutneys, always three.

Prices are on the menu at the counter and include taxes. Dishes can change with the season.

Three chutneys, never four

Coconut, the mild one, with roasted gram. Tomato, the tangy one, with garlic. Coriander, the fresh one, ground green. Podi is a dry powder served alongside: roasted lentil and chilli, eaten with ghee. It is not a chutney.
